I am an assistant professor at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/)’s [Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation](https://fiiai.fudan.edu.cn/) and a PhD supervisor in finance at Fudan’s School of Economics. I recruit doctoral students in green finance, risk management, and sustainable development. I also serve as secretary of the Resource and Environmental Economics Committee of the China Economics Annual Conference. I received my PhD in economics from the [National School of Development](https://www.nsd.pku.edu.cn/) at [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/) in 2022 and was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Faculty of Business and Economics](https://www.hkubs.hku.hk/) at the [University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/) from 2022 to 2026. I have also been a visiting scholar at Yale University and a short-term consultant at the World Bank. My research focuses on environmental, health, and development economics. I have published nine papers in journals including the *Journal of Environmental Economics and Management* (two papers), the *Journal of Population Economics*, *The Journal of World Economy* (two papers), and *China Population, Resources and Environment* (three papers). I lead a Young Scientists Fund (Category C) project supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China. My honors include selection for the youth track of the Shanghai Magnolia Talent Program, the Best Doctoral Dissertation Award from the Chinese Association of Environmental and Resource Economists, the Best Student Paper Award from the Chinese Economists Society, two Outstanding Paper Awards at the China Health Economic Development Forum, and the Outstanding Paper Award at the 8th Annual Conference of the China Labor Economists Forum.

## Research Interests

- Green Finance (green insurance; ESG)
- Environmental Economics (environmental policy; climate change; water pollution; air pollution)
- Health Economics (environmental health; nutrition; health policy)
- Development Economics (aid; human capital)
