Hi! I am a tenure-track assistant professor at the [Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation](https://fiiai.fudan.edu.cn/) at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/). I received my bachelor’s degree from Shandong University in 2017 and my Ph.D. in economics from the [National School of Development](https://www.nsd.pku.edu.cn/) at [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/) in 2022. Before joining Fudan, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Faculty of Business and Economics](https://www.hkubs.hku.hk/) at the [University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/). My research covers green finance, environmental economics, health economics, and development economics. Grounded in China’s real-world problems, I combine scientific methods with economic theory and causal identification to study how pollution, climate change, and environmental policy affect firms, health, and social welfare. My work has been published in the *Journal of Environmental Economics and Management*, the *Journal of Population Economics*, *The Journal of World Economy* (世界经济), and *China Population, Resources and Environment* (中国人口·资源与环境).

## Research Interests

- Green Finance (green insurance; ESG)
- Environmental Economics (environmental policy; climate change; water pollution; air pollution)
- Health Economics (environmental health; nutrition; health policy)
- Development Economics (aid; human capital)
