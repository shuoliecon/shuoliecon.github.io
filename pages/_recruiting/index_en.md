<p class="site-notice"><strong>I am recruiting Ph.D. students this year (School of Economics, Fudan University; Applied Economics and Finance).</strong><br/>
Research directions: <strong>Green Finance; Insurance and Risk Management; Sustainable Development</strong></p>

## Ph.D. Students

I am happy to hear from prospective doctoral students whose interests overlap with my research. Strong candidates typically have solid training in economics and econometrics, curiosity about policy-relevant questions, and the motivation to develop an independent research agenda.

To express interest, please email me a CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of your research interests. You are welcome to include a research paper or other writing sample. Formal admission is handled through Fudan University's doctoral admissions process for the relevant year.

## Research Assistants

Students in economics, public policy, statistics, data science, or related fields are welcome to apply for research assistant positions. Typical work includes literature reviews, data collection and cleaning, geospatial data processing, econometric analysis, visualization, and replication.

I look for careful and reliable work, clear communication, and proficiency in at least one of Stata, R, or Python. Experience with GIS, machine learning, or large datasets is especially useful.

To apply, please send a CV and transcript with a short note on your research interests and weekly availability. A code sample, course project, or other example of research work is also welcome.

## Contact

Please send materials to [lishuo@fudan.edu.cn](mailto:lishuo@fudan.edu.cn) with the subject line “PhD Inquiry” or “RA Application”. A short, specific email describing your fit is more helpful than a generic message.
