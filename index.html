Hi! I am a tenure-track assistant professor at the Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation at Fudan University. I obtained my Ph.D. in economics from the National School of Development at Peking University and completed my postdoctoral training at the Faculty of Business and Economics at the University of Hong Kong.

I am an applied microeconomist working on environmental, health, and development issues. My research is characterized by a focus on real-world problems, a commitment to informing policy, and a dedication to interdisciplinary collaboration.

My work has been published in the Journal of Environmental Economics and Management, the Journal of Population Economics, The Journal of World Economy (世界经济), and China Population, Resources and Environment (中国人口·资源与环境).

Research Interests

  • Green Finance (green insurance; ESG)
  • Environmental Economics (environmental policy; climate change; water pollution; air pollution)
  • Health Economics (environmental health; nutrition; health policy)
  • Development Economics (aid; human capital)
